 Water Merchant Overview and Setup
 The Water Merchant is one of the starting merchant classes in Probably Stolen, built around purifying toxic water and selling clean bottles to customers. Your starting loadout is consistent across every run: a jug of toxic water, a HydroTech purifier with limited charges, and basic containers. Customers only begin purchasing at Base Water quality or higher , so your first job is turning toxic stock into sellable product.

 The perk selection at setup matters more than it looks. Working Pipes gives you 600 credits of starting value , while Savings and Short Sleeper keep your income flowing and prevent missed customers. Bad Lease raises starting rent by 40, a small cost for the value the other perks provide.
 Setup Tip
 Use Normal Markup and skip the intro. Higher markups slow early sales, and early credits matter more than margin when you're racing toward your first purifiers.

 Days 1-4: The Purifier Rush
 The opening days follow a tight script. Ignore wholesale buying entirely and funnel every credit into purifiers. On day one, purify your full jug into Base Water, split off 1,500 ml into a second bottle, and use remaining HydroTech charges to purify that too. Place the jug in the display case to attract buyers, sell as much water as possible to your first customer, and buy their empty bottles since you cannot craft containers yourself.
 Do Not Buy Wholesale Early
 Random deals appear in the first few days, especially Jackson's friend discount. You need liquid credits to grab them. Spending on stock early locks you out of the purifier rush.
 1
 Day 1 - Purify and Sell
 Use all HydroTech charges to create Base Water, display the jug, sell to your first customer, and buy their empty bottles for future use.
 2
 Day 1 Night - Buy From Jackson
 Check Jackson for a purifier (roughly 20-25% chance per visit). Move the battery from the bottle maker into a new purifier, fill a standard bottle with rust water from the faucet, and slot it in.
 3
 Day 2 - Sell Out and Watch for Deals
 Sell remaining Base Water, and buy Jackson's friend discount on three modules when offered. Leave Odin Haraldson alone if he appears; he's useful later.
 4
 Day 3 - Flip the Modules
 Place only the modules in the display case. The low-level inventor is the ideal buyer. Sell the efficiency and quality modules, but keep the performance module for your future security system.
 5
 Day 3-4 - Buy From the Water Merchant
 When the traveling Water Merchant appears, buy his purifier. He always stocks one, unlike Jackson. Buy a battery at night and keep a bottle of rust water inside purifying.

 Power Shortages
 Events like power shortages spike battery prices. If one hits, wait it out rather than overpaying; shop restocks and events shift timing, but a purifier-only focus keeps variance low.
 Daily Loop and Income
 Once all three purifiers are online, the Water Merchant settles into a stable rhythm. Each purifier converts one standard bottle of rust water into high-quality water every night, and each bottle sells for 50 credits, giving you roughly 150 credits per day from three purifiers.

 Bulk Bottle Trick
 Click and drag from outside the bottle boxes to highlight multiple bottles at once, then drag the group to the rust water faucet. This fills every selected bottle at once and saves significant time once you run three purifiers.
 Two side income sources are worth activating during the ramp-up:
 Appraisal Board : Pay the joining fee when approached, then sell the extra magnifying glass to the black-suited buyer who purchases without markup. Displaying the appraisal item generates extra income with no ongoing cost.
 Module flipping : Friend-discount modules resell at a large profit to module buyers, netting around 160 credits in a typical run.
 Rent, Insurance, and Security
 Rent is the Water Merchant's main threat during the purifier rush. Balancing purifier and battery purchases against rent payments requires constant attention, especially when random events drain your budget.
 Insurance From Day 4
 Start buying insurance from day four onward. Without it, theft events can remove your purifiers or stock, undoing days of progress during your most vulnerable phase.
 After your three purifiers are running, the next upgrade should be a security system using the performance module (kept back from the module flip). A properly built security setup removes the need to keep paying for insurance, converting a recurring cost into a one-time investment.

 FAQ
 Q: What is the best Water Merchant start in Probably Stolen?
 Pick Working Pipes, Savings, and Short Sleeper with Bad Lease, use Normal Markup, then rush three water purifiers by day 8-14 without buying any other wholesale stock.
 Q: Where do I get water purifiers?
 Two sources: Jackson stocks one roughly 20-25% of the time you visit, while the traveling Water Merchant always carries one. Check Jackson every night and buy from the Water Merchant whenever he appears.
 Q: How much money does the Water Merchant make per day?
 Each purifier produces one bottle of high-quality water per night, selling for 50 credits. With three purifiers running, expect around 150 credits per day.
 Q: Should I interact with Odin Haraldson early?
 No. On a first playthrough, let him pass. He becomes genuinely useful later in the run, so avoid any early confrontation.
