 How do perk points work?
 The starting-perk screen follows specialization selection on later runs; the first run begins without perk selection. You have three available points and four slots . A drawback can provide more points while still occupying a slot.
 In this table, positive numbers cost points , negative numbers grant points , and zero is a free trade-off . Effect entries are short summaries; read the in-game tooltip before committing, especially when perks conflict.

 Which perks come from faction reputation?
 Reputation effects activate during a run rather than spending starting points. Positive thresholds are benefits, negative thresholds are penalties; Honor Amongst Thieves applies at every standing.  Which benefits fit your business?
 Think about the problem you want the perk to solve. Extra starting money helps an opening purchase; protection helps preserve stock; a connection helps an intended specialty. A perk that is valuable for a scavenging run may do little for a shop that never makes an after-hours trip.
 Compare a loadout against the starting scenario rather than choosing several attractive effects in isolation. Each slot should support a job you actually plan to do.
 Which drawbacks deserve extra care?
 A small point gain can add a large obligation. Rent increases compete with early investment. Withdrawal affects trading. A reduced inventory can make a production business harder to manage. A survival requirement follows you into every busy day.
 The official 1.0 preview specifically advises against Sentinel Collapse Disorder for a first run. Some challenge perks also connect to run-ending outcomes. Those details stay inside the collapsed panels of the endings guide .
 For a low-friction opening, learn the beginner routine before adding several constraints. Keep the money guide nearby when comparing an immediate bonus with a recurring cost.
