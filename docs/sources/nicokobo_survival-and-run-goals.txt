Wounds, addiction and treatment
State
Rule and treatment
Minor / major wound
Adds 2 / 5 wound points and makes the wound unstable; severe means strictly above 5
Unstable wound
Each night first checks a 10% chance to worsen by 1; only if that fails does it separately check a 50% chance to stabilize
Stable wound
Normally loses 4 points per night, minimum 0; an unstable wound above 10 has a special branch that caps it, stabilizes it and subtracts 1
Bandage
Requires an unstable wound; an ordinary bandage stabilizes it, while hemostatic and topical bandages also subtract 1; the item is consumed
Nicotine / alcohol addiction
With addiction already present, 3 days since use triggers the penalty; reaching 20 removes that addiction
Drug addiction
The smaller of the days-since-use and days-since-relief counters must reach 3 for the penalty; removal still requires 20 days since use
Sentinel Collapse Disorder
At 7 / 10 / 14 days since injection, the disease pool increases by 1 / 2 / 3 per night; reaching 20 triggers its ending
Tilapia Skin Graft
 / 
unfertilized Graft Tilapia Roe
Requires ownership and an unstable wound; stabilizes it and subtracts 2 / 3 respectively, minimum 0. Fertilized eggs cannot be used this way
Immunivax
Genuine resets days since injection to 0; expired sets 3; counterfeit sets a random 3–5. Unusable items or those without the authenticity feature do not reset it but are consumed. None reduces the accumulated disease pool
Each active withdrawal increases the player’s buying markup by 8 percentage points and reduces the player’s selling markup by 8 percentage points at the market-price stage; this is not a fixed 8% change to the final quote. Tobacco and alcohol directly reset their corresponding counters; narcotic-use and relief counters each update using the applicable lower value. The days-since-relief counter increments only below 999 and stops at 999. It does not replace the 20-day abstinence requirement for removing addiction. See 
trading and bargaining
 for full price calculations. Do not apply these periods to gambling addiction. Drug effects and complete disease thresholds are in

Saving and historical saves
The first save follows starting-stock and perk initialization.
Confirming the end of the day runs overnight processing. The Sentinel ending appears during the night without terminating subsequent processing. After entering the new day, the reactor ending is checked before the Aug ending; only if neither branch applies does this automatic-save step run.
Select a slot in the load interface and expand its available historical days to load one.
The history setting is 1–10, including the latest save. Its default when no setting has been saved is 3: the latest save and at most two historical saves, not three additional saves. Retry and recovery mechanisms exist, but do not guarantee recovery from every failure.

The three cumulative counters are Steam account statistics: correct reports, Aug shootings and perfect appraisals. With Steam statistics ready, the handlers read the old value and add one, unlocking at ten; they stop incrementing after the corresponding achievement is unlocked. These handlers skip when statistics are not ready and contain no offline queue.
Run-summary statistics are serialized with the current run and are separate from Steam account counters. Shop valuation adds the specified owned-item values to cash; the machine achievement counts machine objects, rather than power or output. The corresponding production, appraisal, reputation and trading rules remain with their own topics.
Source: 0.46D, Build 26337366; static analysis, not an in-game test.
