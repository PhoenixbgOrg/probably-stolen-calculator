 Probably Stolen Water Basics: Grades and Purification
 In Probably Stolen, the water merchant run revolves around turning free toxic rust water from your faucet into sellable product. Customers only start buying at base water quality or higher , and every grade above that improves your margins. The purification chain is the backbone of the entire build.

 Start With the HydroTech Purifier
 On day one, use the HydroTech purifier on the entire jug to make base water, then split 1,500 ml into a separate bottle and purify the remainder. Every charge counts early.
 Best Starting Perks for a Water Merchant Run
 Perk selection defines your early economy. The goal is maximum starting value and no missed customers, even at the cost of slightly higher rent.
 Working Pipes

 Savings
 Extra starting credits for purifier purchases
 Short Sleeper

 Trivial cost versus perk value gained
 Use normal markup and skip the intro to accelerate the setup phase.
 Do Not Buy Wholesale Early
 Resist spending on anything besides purifiers, batteries, and rent. Discount deals appear regularly, and you need liquid credits to grab purifiers the moment they show up.
 Day-by-Day Water Merchant Route
 1
 Day 1: First Sales and Bottle Stock
 Purify the starting jug to base water, place it in the display case, and sell to your first customer. Buy their empty bottles since you cannot manufacture them yet. Skip other purchases entirely.
 2
 Day 2: First Purifier Online
 Visit Jackson and buy a purifier if stocked. Move the battery from the bottle maker into it, fill a standard bottle with rust water, and slot it in. Let Odin Haraldson pass untouched — he matters later.
 3
 Day 3: Flip Discount Modules
 When Jackson offers a friend discount on three modules, buy it. Sell the efficiency and quality modules to the low-level inventor for a big profit (~160 credits), but keep the performance module for a future security system.
 4
 Day 4: Second and Third Purifiers
 The traveling water merchant always carries a purifier — buy it. Keep checking Jackson nightly (roughly a 20-25% chance he stocks one). Buy batteries only when prices are normal; power shortages inflate them badly.
 5
 Day 5-14: Core Loop
 Every morning, pour out high-quality water into main containers, refill empty bottles with rust water using drag-select, and sell out your stock. Join the appraisal board when offered and sell the spare magnifying glass for bonus income.
 Drag-Select Trick
 Click and drag from outside the bottle boxes to highlight multiple bottles at once, then drag the group to the rust water faucet. This saves significant time once you run three purifiers.

 Risk Management: Rent, Events, and Theft
 Random events and shop restocks shift the strategy's timing, but the purifier-first focus removes most variance. The main threats are rent shortfalls, battery price spikes, and theft.
 Risk Cause Countermeasure 
 Missed rent Overspending on machines Track credits before every purchase 
 Battery price spikes Power shortage events Delay battery buys until prices normalize 
 Stolen stock No insurance Buy insurance daily from day 4 onward 
 Repeated insurance cost Ongoing theft risk Build security system (49 performance) 
 Watch Your Rent Buffer
 Balancing purifier purchases, batteries, and events makes it easy to fall behind on rent. Always confirm you can cover the next payment before committing credits.

 FAQ
 Q: What is the best Probably Stolen water merchant start?
 Take Working Pipes, Savings, Short Sleeper, and Bad Lease perks, then spend everything on getting three water purifiers online by day 8-14. Avoid all other wholesale purchases until then.
 Q: Where do I buy water purifiers?
 Jackson stocks them with roughly a 20-25% chance per visit, while the traveling water merchant always carries one. Check Jackson every night after closing.
 Q: How much money do three purifiers generate?
 Each purifier turns a standard bottle of rust water into high-quality water overnight. At 50 credits per bottle, three purifiers yield about 150 credits per day.
 Q: Should I keep any of the discount modules?
 Sell the efficiency and quality modules for profit, but keep the performance module — it is required for the security alarm system you should build later.
