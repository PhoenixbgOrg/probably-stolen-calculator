 Quick Guide

 Core loop : buy rats, feed them high-calorie food, breed them, harvest rat meat
 Key tools : animal cage, livestock feed dispenser, gene scanner, meat cleaver
 Serums matter : growth serum speeds maturing; immunity serum prevents disease losses
 Watch calories : regular Neutrfruit (300 kcal) beats small Neutrfruit for feeding
 Rat Wrangler Overview: Why Ranch Rats?
 Rat ranching is one of the most surprising and profitable side activities in Probably Stolen. Beyond your main shopkeeping, smelting, and contraband deals, the animal cage system lets you raise, breed, and eventually process rats into valuable raw meat. It is available in the demo version even when most other vendor options are locked, making it an excellent early income stream.

 Why It Works
 Rat meat sells for a solid price, and rats breed on their own once you have a mixed population. A well-fed cage essentially converts cheap Neutrfruit into sellable meat over time.

 Battery Check
 The gene scanner and dispenser need battery power. A dead battery mid-cycle means stats vanish and feeding stalls, so keep a spare on hand.
 Step-by-Step Ranch Setup
 1
 Acquire the Cage
 Unlock rat ranching from the vendor (it is one of the few non-demo-locked options). Place the cage and expand it early with metal ingots, more space means less crowding and more breeding room.
 2
 Stock the Feed Dispenser
 Load regular Neutrfruit (300 kcal each) rather than small Neutrfruit when possible. Fill the dispenser and let it auto-feed. Do not manually empty it, that dumps your stored calories.
 3
 Buy and Label Rats
 Purchase rats when the price is marked down, and negotiate it down further. Label them to track bloodlines. A mixed male/female population will begin breeding naturally.
 4
 Apply Serums Carefully
 Growth serum raises growth rate but makes rats immunocompromised. Follow up with immunity serum to fix the status. Diseased rats can be treated or culled.
 5
 Harvest for Meat
 Use the meat cleaver on dying, diseased, or surplus rats. Each rat yields rat meat that sells for a respectable price, or can be fed back to the colony in emergencies.
 Expansion Priority
 Bigger cages are almost always worth the metal. Crowded rats live in filth faster than a cleaning robot can manage, and disease spreads through tight populations.
 Common Problems and Fixes

 Starvation Losses
 If your food stock runs dry, rats die hungry and yield less meat. During water-supply crises, food prices spike, so keep a reserve of Neutrfruit, cheese, or even ramen (high calorie) to grind through shortages.
 Profit Strategy and Daily Routine
 Treat the rat ranch as a passive income engine layered on top of your shop. Rats breed, eat, and grow while you handle customers, smelting, and scavenging runs.
 Daily Ranch Checklist:
 Check feed dispenser calorie reserve 
 Remove feces or verify cleaning robot 
 Scan new rats and apply serums as needed 
 Harvest diseased or dying rats for meat 
 Negotiate rat prices down before buying 
 Queue metal ingots for cage expansion 
 Meat economics work best when you pair harvesting with a food or raw-meat buyer visiting your counter. Raw meat fits neatly into large food budgets, and a single big-budget customer can absorb an entire harvest.
 Insider Trading
 Pair the ranch with market events. When a water treatment breakdown is announced, stockpile water and remember that beer counts as a beverage during shortages, freeing your food stock entirely for the rats.
 FAQ
 Q: How do I unlock rat ranching in Probably Stolen?
 Rat ranching is available through the vendor even in the demo version, where most other unlocks are unavailable. Build up favor with the vendor and purchase the animal cage to begin.
 Q: What is the best food for rats?
 Regular Neutrfruit at 300 kcal is the most efficient staple feed. Small Neutrfruit works but stores fewer calories per unit. In a pinch, cheese, ramen, and even ground-up candy bars keep rats from starving.
 Q: Should I use growth serum on my rats?
 Yes, but follow it with immunity serum. Growth serum raises the growth rate but leaves rats immunocompromised, and untreated rats are vulnerable to disease outbreaks in the cage.
 Q: What do I do with diseased rats?
 You can attempt treatment with immunity serum, but diseased or dying rats are often most valuable harvested. Use the meat cleaver to convert them into rat meat, which sells for a solid price to food buyers.
