 Probably Stolen Starting Scenarios: 9 Launch Choices
 Compare all nine selectable starts and learn that first-run perks are unavailable; checked October 28, 2026 against Steam build 26263409.
 Christian Kuri • Oct 28, 2026 • 6 MIN READ 
 Share 
 Checked October 28, 2026 against Steam build 26263409, this chooser compares nine launch starts by specialty and the opening equipment the game lists. Choose a scenario for the trade or challenge you want, then select difficulty separately; starting perks are unavailable on a first run. Recheck this comparison after patches that change starts, labels, or difficulty.
 The nine launch starts give you different opening setups
 The checked reference lists nine playable starts. Their official specialties describe the kind of trade or challenge each run is built around; the separate equipment column records what the launch-build data lists you as starting with. Labels appear only where the reference supplies one.
 A Gunsmith run opens with firearms, gun mods and ammunition alongside its Class I permit.

 The launch roster is smaller than the game-data list
 Checked October 28, 2026 against Steam build 26263409, the launch roster is nine playable starts: Apprentice, Water Merchant, Fence, Rock Bottom, Hydroponic Farmer, Gunsmith, Chemist, Rat Wrangler, and Aquafarmer. That matches the nine scenarios in the developer’s October 21 prelaunch overview. The October 28 launch update repeats the nine-start count; its text is mirrored by SteamDB.
 The 1.0 launch paths include specialist trades such as the Gunsmith's; the dated roster separates those nine starts from later plans. 
 The more detailed availability check comes from a third-party release-build audit, which reports reading build 26263409 and marks nine scenario definitions “Playable.” It lists three others as “Work In Progress”—Junker, Organ Dealer, and Street Food Vendor—and two more, Moonshiner and Pharmacist, as absent from the start screen. A scenario definition in the game’s data is not by itself a selectable start.
 The official announcements call the planned organ scenario “Organ Trader,” while the data page calls its WIP entry “Organ Dealer.” Those are the respective sources’ labels; the available evidence does not resolve the naming difference. Both the launch-day update and prelaunch overview place the announced future starts outside the nine available at launch.
 The prelaunch and release-build rosters therefore agree on which nine starts launch, and the scenario subjects remain consistent. The release-build reference adds a more specific availability status than the preview could provide. For a launch run, use the nine dated playable entries rather than a demo roster or a WIP/hidden definition.
 Choose difficulty and perks after the scenario
 The scenario’s label does not set your difficulty. Rock Bottom is marked “Extreme Challenge Mode,” but the launch build offers a separate choice of four settings: Price Gouge (Cozy Mode), Mark Up (Normal Mode), Thin Margins (Hard Mode), and Red Ink (Challenge Mode). The launch announcement makes the distinction explicit by suggesting Rock Bottom together with the super-hard setting for an added challenge. Treat that as an option the game points out, not a recommendation that every Rock Bottom run must use it.
 Price Gouge, Mark Up and Thin Margins appear on the separate difficulty panel; Red Ink is the fourth launch option, while perks have their own screen. 
 Starting Perks are another separate panel that appears after scenario selection. On a first run, the panel is unavailable, so you cannot configure starting perks yet; the scenario’s listed opening conditions are not preassigned perks.
 Choose the scenario for the trade or challenge in its opening, then select difficulty independently. If this is your first run, proceed without expecting a perk choice.
 Frequently Asked Questions
 1 Can you choose Starting Perks on a first run? 
 No. The Starting Perks panel is unavailable on a first run, so proceed with the scenario’s listed opening conditions.
 2 Are the work-in-progress scenarios playable at launch? 
 No. The checked release-build audit marks Junker, Organ Dealer, and Street Food Vendor as work in progress; the launch roster has nine playable starts.
