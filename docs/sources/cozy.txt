 Probably Stolen Starting Scenarios: All 9 and Which to Pick
 Updated October 23, 2026 
 Probably Stolen has nine starting scenarios in 1.0 : Apprentice, Water Merchant, Fence, Rock Bottom, Hydroponic Farmer, Gunsmith, Chemist, Rat Wrangler and Aquafarmer. Each one sets your first stock and the kind of trouble you'll be dealing with. Below: what each starts with, its main risk, where it came from, and the starting perks named in patch notes. Checked on October 23, 2026 against the developer's posts.

 Quick answer
 New player? Start as the Apprentice . Want a challenge? Rock Bottom starts you with nothing. The Rat Wrangler was the playtest's secret scenario.
 All nine starting scenarios
 Apprentice
 Beginner The generalist start. Learn every system at your own pace and upgrade as you go.
 Starts with: A general store with no specialty Main risk: None in particular: you choose your risks later History: The developer calls it the best start for new players. Water Merchant
 Standard Test, purify and relabel water, honestly or not. Pure water is worth more than gold on the station.
 Starts with: Water testing and purification gear Main risk: Liability: mislabeled or dirty water comes back to you History: Added with the Steam demo on September 14. Fence
 Standard Deal in stolen goods. Move them fast and stay out of Security's sight.
 Starts with: A stolen-goods business Main risk: Raids: contraband in the shop can be found History: Listed on the Steam page since announcement. Rock Bottom
 Hardest Start with nothing at all. The game's maximum difficulty.
 Starts with: Nothing Main risk: Everything: no stock, no tools, rent still rises History: Added in playtest patch 48.3.10 (August 7) as the hardcore start; players named it a favorite in the September playtest survey. Hydroponic Farmer
 Standard Grow crops in a cramped space, ending with genetically modified strains that sell for a fortune, including some people probably shouldn't buy.
 Starts with: Hydroponic machines and farming supplies Main risk: Space and power: crops need room and energy History: Called “farmer” on the Steam page; named Hydroponic Farmer in the 1.0 list. Gunsmith
 Standard Weapons and weapon mods: high margins, regular security inspections.
 Starts with: A Class 1 weapon permit, a stock of firearms and ammunition Main risk: Inspections: weapons draw Security History: Listed on the Steam page since announcement. Chemist
 Standard Profitable chemicals made from closely guarded recipes.
 Starts with: Chemistry lab equipment Main risk: Addiction: chemicals can hook (or hurt) your customers History: Listed on the Steam page since announcement. Rat Wrangler
 Standard Breed rats. The developer's challenge: can you grow a healthy 2×3 rat?
 Starts with: Rats and cages Main risk: Cages wear out, rats breed and escape History: The playtest's secret scenario, hidden on the profession screen; made visible in patch 48.3.12 (August 8). Aquafarmer
 Beginner Breed fish in recirculating tanks. Water quality, food and environment decide what fish you end up with.
 Starts with: A stock of tilapia and recirculating aquaculture systems Main risk: Tank water quality, filtration and energy cells History: Arrived in the demo on October 19 as a Wild's Network upgrade (Retired Aquafarmer); a full starting scenario in 1.0. Difficulty labels are ours, based on the developer's descriptions (“best for beginners”, “maximum difficulty”, “more beginner friendly than rat ranching”). We'll rate the rest once we've played 1.0.

 In 1.0 you pick one of four difficulty options after choosing a scenario. Before 1.0, Hard Mode was the only toggle: it is the one mode where missing too many augs ends the run with the “Augmented” ending.

 Starting perks we know about
 Perks are bought with starting points at the start of a run. These are the ones named in official patch notes (playtest and demo). 1.0 adds more; we'll list them all after launch.
 Perk | What it does (latest patch) |

 Frequently asked questions
 What is the best starting scenario in Probably Stolen for beginners?
 + Apprentice: the developer calls it the best start for new players, a generalist shop where you learn each system at your own pace. Aquafarmer is also described as beginner-friendly: fish need less upkeep than rats.
 What is the secret scenario in Probably Stolen?
 + The Rat Wrangler. It was hidden on the playtest's profession selection screen from day one, and the developer made it visible in patch 48.3.12 on August 8, 2026.
 What is the hardest start?
 + Rock Bottom: you start with nothing, and the developer calls it maximum difficulty. On top of the scenario, 1.0 adds four difficulty options.
 How many starting scenarios does Probably Stolen have?
 + Nine in the 1.0 release. Junker (shortly after launch), Organ Trader and Street Food Vendor are planned as free updates.
 What are starting perks?
 + Traits you buy with starting points when you begin a run. Positive perks such as Intimidating cost points; addiction perks are drawbacks with a trading penalty. 1.0 adds new ones, and the demo added two perks designed for challenge runs.
