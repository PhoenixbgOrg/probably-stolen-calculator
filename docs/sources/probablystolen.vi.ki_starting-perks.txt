 Starting Perks
 Probably Stolen's starting perks unlock from your second run. You get 3 points and 4 slots; negative perks add points. AllThingsHow's money pick is Savings plus Vigilant or Diplomatic.

 Negative perks
 Each gives 1 point : Agoraphobia (rest 2 nights after an outing), Alcohol, Narcotic and Nicotine Addiction (8% trade penalty without the substance, curable), Bad Lease (rent +40), Banned from the Commissary, Convict (-30 Security reputation, smaller rewards), Degenerative Osteoarthritis (fewer searches each week), Gambling Addiction (always buys lottery tickets) and Illiterate (cannot read guides). The studio's official images and notes also show perks outside this list, such as Awful Negotiator , which disables bargaining with a 5% penalty , and Sentinel Collapse Disorder , so counts differ by source.
 Best picks
 1:57 AllThingsHow's money start is Savings plus Vigilant or Diplomatic . GamerBlurb suggests Sawyer's Crew plus Vigilant for water and fish, Intimidating plus Sawyer's Crew for illegal trading, and Model Citizen plus Diplomatic for a Security run . GitM's Rock Bottom used Savings, Sawyer's Crew and Vigilant . ambiguousamphibian's Rock Bottom run in the clip takes Proficient Scavenger, Short Sleeper and Lucky.
 Frequently asked questions
 How do you unlock perks in Probably Stolen? Finish or end your first run; Probably Stolen's perk screen is locked until your second.
 What are the best perks in Probably Stolen? AllThingsHow recommends Savings with Vigilant or Diplomatic for money, and Colorful Character when you want more than four perks.

 Achievements Probably Stolen has 21 Steam achievements, from Lower Levels Special for using the labeler to From Nothing, the Hard Way, held by 0.7% of players on Nov 1, 2026. 
 Beginners Guide Start Probably Stolen as the Apprentice on Mark Up, keep 200 credits back for rent on Day 7, stock food, inspect everything you buy, and only report a wanted face you are sure of. 
 Buying the Store Win Probably Stolen by buying the deed from the landlord on a rent day, 12,000 credits on Mark Up and rising 50 a day to 17,000, or by paying off the Loan Shark's mortgage. Prices, caps and mortgage terms for every difficulty, and what happens after you win. 
 Controls Probably Stolen controls: Q and E rotate, F flips, O shows hints, B rings the bell, Ctrl-click transfers, a drag selects many, and 1 to 9 pick tools from the Toolbox. 
 How to Make Money In Probably Stolen, make money early by flipping food and household goods and scavenging, then appraisals and the furnace, then water, wine, fish or batteries, and weapons late. Build one working business before buying more machines. 
 Rent Rent in Probably Stolen is 200 credits every seven days, rising 2%, 5% or 10% each time by difficulty. Refusing with no grace left ends the run. 
 Aquafarming Aquafarming in Probably Stolen means breeding fish in tanks, tilapia first and then eels and sturgeon, for meat and caviar. The studio calls it slower-paced and more beginner-friendly than Rat Ranching. 
 Machines and Production Probably Stolen's machines run overnight and draw power: the Furnace turns scrap and ore into ingots, the wine setup ferments and ages, Hydroponics grow crops, and a 3D Printer makes weapons.

 Augs Augs in Probably Stolen are people taken over by a rogue AI chip. Signs are a visible chip, odd pupils, an uncanny expression, stillness, twitching, robotic or formal speech and an outfit that does not match their faction. 
 Difficulty Modes Probably Stolen has four difficulties: Price Gouge (Cozy), Mark Up (Normal), Thin Margins (Hard) and Red Ink (Challenge). They set your retail markup, rent growth, permit price and reputation gains. 
 Factions and Reputation Probably Stolen has six factions: Lower Levels, Upper Levels, Security, Black Market, Revolution and the Sector 13 Cartel. Standing changes prices and access, and -100 with most of them ends your run. 
 Security and Contraband Security in Probably Stolen inspects at random and builds a case from your crimes: 300 credits of evidence means arrest and 1,500 means execution. Hide contraband in a Smuggler's Bay, or bribe. 
 Starting Scenarios Probably Stolen has nine starting scenarios: Apprentice, Water Merchant, Fence, Gunsmith, Chemist, Hydroponic Farmer, Aquafarmer, Rat Wrangler and Rock Bottom. Pick Apprentice first. 
 Wanted List Probably Stolen's Wanted poster has nine names worth 3,600 credits, from Lou at 1,500 to Arthur Lafleur at 100. Hold the teal Call Security button only when you are certain of the face.
