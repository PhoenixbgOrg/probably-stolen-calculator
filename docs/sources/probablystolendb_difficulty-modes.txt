Quick Answer
Apprentice on Price Gouge gives you the gentlest costs and reputation rules. Mark Up is the default balance.

Choosing a mode
Price Gouge (Cozy)
 offers the widest resale margin and mildest reputation penalties. 
Mark Up (Normal)
 is the default. Both suit an 
Apprentice
 first run with the tutorial enabled.
Thin Margins (Hard)
 removes the retail markup. Profit comes from discounted purchases and production, with higher costs for network upgrades. 
Red Ink (Challenge)
 keeps those tight margins and raises costs and reputation penalties further.
Choose your 
starting scenario
 separately. For 
From Nothing, the Hard Way
, win with 
Rock Bottom on Thin Margins or Red Ink
. The 
Rock Bottom run
 covers the income that start needs before it owns any stock. Other run goals are in the 
achievement table
.

Rent Grace
 carries one missed payment into the next week’s bill. Price Gouge and Mark Up include it at the start; on Thin Margins and Red Ink, buy it through 
Joe Wild
.
Permit and deed prices
The 
Business Permit
 is cheapest on Price Gouge. The opening deed price is the same on Price Gouge and Mark Up, then rises at different rates after day 50.

Skip Intro and Endless Mode
Skip Intro
 removes introductory encounters and opens content immediately. Leave it off if you want the tutorial and 
First Steps
.
Endless Mode
 changes which scripted customers and events appear. Use a regular run to follow character storylines.
