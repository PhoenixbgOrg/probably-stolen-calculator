 Water Quality System Basics
 This Probably Stolen guide starts with the foundation: understanding how water quality works. The game mimics real-life purification, where every tool targets specific contaminants. Water progresses through tiers, from rust water on tap up to base water, high-quality water, and finally pure water. Knowing which tool handles which contaminant is the single most important concept for efficient purification.

 Common Mistake
 Running base water through a filter alone does not produce high-quality water. The microbes survive because filters never touch them. You must pair a filter with a UV lamp (or use a machine purifier) to clear every contaminant category.
 Buyer Water Shortcut
 Purchased scavenger or seller water has a mixture that responds perfectly to the UV lamp plus filter combo. Your self-made base water may behave differently depending on the path you took, so always test with the aqua scan before committing.

 Aqua Scan Is Your Friend
 Use the aqua scan regularly on any tank or system. It reveals remaining contaminants so you know exactly which tool to apply next instead of guessing.
 Step-by-Step: Making Pure Water
 Pure water is the top tier and only two machines can produce it. Both routes depend on machine quality, not just the purification process itself. Follow these steps:
 1
 Secure a Rust Water Supply
 Fill a jug from your faucet or buy base water from sellers. Purchased water mixes predictably with the UV and filter combo.
 2
 Pre-Filter to Base Water
 Run the water through a filter pitcher to strip organic waste, minerals, and physical debris, leaving only microbes behind.
 3
 Raise Machine Quality to 100%+
 Slot quality modules into your Water Purifier. Hover the tooltip and confirm max purity 99.9% before proceeding. Use up to two expansion kits to open extra module bays.
 4
 Run Overnight Purification Cycles
 Pour the jug into the purifier. A fully charged purifier triggers a full day cycle instantly, or sleep through the night. Expect roughly three nights from rust water to pure water depending on machine performance.
 5
 Verify with Aqua Scan
 Test the water. At 99.9% purity it registers as pure water, ready for storage or premium sale.
 Alternative: Moisture Farm Route
 Drop a refinement module into your Moisture Farm to push quality past the 150% threshold. At 150%+ the farm generates pure water daily, around 578 ml per day in tested setups, fully passive income.

 Machine Optimization & Quality Modules
 Hitting the quality thresholds is where most players stall. The default machine layout leaves empty module bays, and that wasted space is your biggest optimization opportunity.
 Expansion kits can be used twice , opening two additional module bays per machine for quality, efficiency, or performance modules.
 Quality modules push machines past the 100% (purifier) or 150% (moisture farm) thresholds for pure water.
 Efficiency modules lower energy cost, useful when running purifiers every night.
 Performance modules speed up purification, so larger jugs finish within a single night.
 Placement Controls
 Click a machine and press E or Q to rotate it. Right-click to flip it. Use these controls to fit expansion modules and tanks into tight base layouts.

 Storage and Selling Strategy
 Once you produce high-quality and pure water consistently, storage and sales become the profit engine.
 Storage: Water tanks start small but can be upgraded to a 50,000 ml maximum . A 24,000 ml tank is a solid mid-game target, but as daily pure water output grows, maxing storage keeps surplus from going to waste.
 Selling: Place two bottles of spring water on your sale shelf. Each bottle carries a high base value, and together they reliably attract an upper-level Water Stockpiler buyer from around day 14 onward, paying 500+ credits and buying your entire high-quality or pure stock.
 Water Business Milestones:
 Establish a steady rust water or purchased base water supply 
 Build a UV lamp and filter pitcher for quick high-quality batches 
 Upgrade Water Purifier quality to 100%+ with modules 
 Push Moisture Farm quality to 150% for daily pure water 
 Max a water tank at 50,000 ml for stockpiling 
 Display two spring water bottles to trigger the Water Stockpiler buyer 
 Spring Water Alternatives
 If you cannot find a second spring water bottle (one comes from Natalia's quest line, another may drop from random crates), substitute a standard high-quality water bottle. Any displayed combination exceeding roughly 365 total water value triggers the stockpiler.
 FAQ
 Q: Why does my filtered water stay at base quality?
 Filters only remove organic waste, minerals, and physical debris. Microbes survive filtering, so you must also apply a UV lamp or use a water purifier machine to clear every contaminant category and reach high quality.
 Q: What is the fastest way to get pure water in Probably Stolen?
 Use a Water Purifier machine raised to 100%+ quality with quality modules, then run overnight cycles. From rust water, expect roughly three nights. Alternatively, push a Moisture Farm to 150% quality for passive daily pure water.
 Q: How do I hit the 100% or 150% machine quality thresholds?
 Slot quality-focused modules into your machine and use expansion kits, which can each be used once for up to two extra module bays. Check the machine tooltip to confirm purity limits like 99.9% before running cycles.
 Q: How do I attract the Water Stockpiler buyer?
 Display two bottles of spring water on your sales shelf. One comes from Natalia's quest line and another can drop from random crates. From around day 14, this reliably spawns an upper-level buyer who purchases all your high-quality or pure water for 500+ credits.
