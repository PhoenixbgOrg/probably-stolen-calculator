Occupation initialization values
Cash and first rent are in Credits before perk effects. Reputation figures are the career input values; relevant perk and difficulty modifiers can change them, since selected perks already affect ordinary reputation changes during career initialization. Counts are item stacks, not individual ammunition rounds. Later tutorial trades and gifts are excluded.

Selection rules
Perks are disabled on the first run. Later starts must satisfy both: total signed cost ≤3, and selected count ≤4 plus all selected extra slots. Negative costs offset positive costs; zero-cost perks still occupy a slot. Selection may temporarily exceed a limit, but Continue validates it.
Colorful Character is neutral and costs 1 point; it occupies one slot and raises the total slot limit from 4 to 7. Category is not determined by the sign of its cost.

Application order
The game initializes the base state, handles Hoarder placeholders, grants occupation stock and some perk items, handles the skipped introduction, then applies the remaining starting perks and saves for the first time. Occupation cash and rent are set first: Rock Bottom with Savings gets 150 after cash is reset to zero; Fence with Bad Lease gets 250 +40 rent.
Skipping the introduction has separate guide and stock branches. It does not grant every occupation the same additional items.
Source: 0.46D, Build 26337366; static analysis, not an in-game test.
