 Probably Stolen Best Perks: Full Tier List Overview
 Choosing your starting perks in Probably Stolen is one of the most important decisions of any run. Perk points are limited, so every point spent needs to pull its weight across the early survival weeks and the late-game economy. This tier list ranks every positive starting perk based on cost efficiency, early-game impact, and long-term value.

 Avoid This Trap
 Intimidating is the most expensive perk in the game at 3 perk points, yet most builds never struggle with evidence. Joe Wild's fixes and alarm systems handle security reputation far more efficiently. Treat it as the worst perk in Probably Stolen.

 Best value per perk point in the game
 Diplomatic is arguably the strongest perk overall. Reputation is the core engine of every run: it activates faction perks, raises customer budgets, and directly increases daily earnings. Because trading is your main reputation source anyway, the 33% bonus applies constantly.
 Sawyer's Crew saves money from day one. Normally you skip utilities like janitor service in the first week to conserve credits — this perk makes it free, freeing up cash for insurance or power. Even without the Underground Exchange access, it would still rank S-tier on the janitor savings alone.

 Build Priority
 If you only have 2 perk points, take Sawyer's Crew plus one more. If you have 4 points, Sawyer's Crew and Diplomatic together form the strongest opening combination in the game.
 A-Tier and B-Tier Perks: Strong Support Picks
 A-Tier: Vigilant, Well-Connected, Welfare Program 
 Vigilant eliminates night robberies entirely, which matters most in the critical first two weeks when protecting your goods decides whether you survive. The only drawback is that buying insurance daily builds Joe Wild's favor faster than this perk does — but in the late game, saved insurance premiums on a high-net-worth store add up quickly.
 Well-Connected gives +15 starting reputation across every faction, putting you within 5 points of key lower-level and security perks like the extra normal customer and the security merchant unlock.
 Welfare Program starts you with a food stamp worth 160 commissary tokens (about 120 credits if sold, but worth far more spent on food). Buy commissary food on day one, resell it day two, and jump-start your entire economy.

 Rock Bottom Note
 On Rock Bottom starts with zero credits, Savings jumps to S-tier value — 150 credits means you can trade on day one. Similarly, Welfare Program is exceptionally strong when you begin with nothing.
 How to Pick Perks for Your Run
 1
 Lock In the S-Tier Core
 Spend your first points on Sawyer's Crew (1 point) and Diplomatic (2 points). This combination covers economy, reputation, and utilities for the lowest cost.
 2
 Add Survival Coverage
 With points remaining, take Vigilant for night protection on normal runs, or Welfare Program for an economic jump start on Rock Bottom.
 3
 Consider the Slot Combo
 For hardest challenge runs, take Colorful Character (3 extra slots, net 2) paired with Minimalist (-3 points cost, smaller inventory) to net extra perk points for stronger picks.
 4
 Fill Flex Slots
 Round out with Model Citizen for security builds or Well-Connected for general reputation. Skip Intimidating regardless of build.

 Remember the Satchel
 When using Welfare Program, bring a satchel to the commissary. You only start with two pockets, and you cannot carry back a full haul of food with base inventory space.
 Perk Synergies and Common Mistakes
 The strongest perk interactions in Probably Stolen come from pairing reputation and economy perks. Model Citizen's +30 security reputation pairs with the smuggler's bay strategy — higher security reputation means less frequent inspections, protecting your contraband pipeline. The Colorful Character plus Minimalist combo effectively trades a smaller inventory for an extra positive perk, which is worth it only if the extra perk is a strong one.
 Common mistakes to avoid:
 Taking Intimidating and wasting 3 points on an effect alarm systems replicate cheaper
 Taking Tape Head over Savings — the tapes net roughly 190 credits but cannot be sold until day seven, while Savings gives 150 usable on day one
 Spending all commissary tokens without inventory space to carry the food home
 Skipping insurance entirely late game when your store's net worth drives up theft risk
 Tape Head vs Savings
 Tape Head yields about 40 credits more than Savings overall, but the 7-day delay kills its value. Day-one capital compounds through buying and reselling inventory, so earlier money is always worth more.

 FAQ
 Q: What are the best perks in Probably Stolen?
 Diplomatic and Sawyer's Crew are the consensus S-tier picks. Diplomatic boosts all trade reputation by 33% with no downside, while Sawyer's Crew provides free janitor service and Underground Exchange access for just 1 perk point.
 Q: Is Intimidating worth taking?
 No. At 3 perk points it is the most expensive perk in the game, and most builds never struggle with evidence generation. Alarm systems and Joe Wild's fixes handle security concerns far more efficiently.
 Q: Which perks are best for a Rock Bottom start?
 Welfare Program and Savings shine here. Welfare Program's 160 commissary tokens let you buy food on day one and resell it for a huge head start, while 150 starting credits enables immediate trading.
 Q: Does Colorful Character give extra perk slots?
 It grants 3 additional slots but uses one itself, netting 2 extra slots. Since you typically need a negative perk to fill them, pair it with Minimalist, which refunds 3 perk points for a smaller inventory.
