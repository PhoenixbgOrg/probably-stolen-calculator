 Probably Stolen Starting Perks Guide
 All 48 starting perks in Probably Stolen with their exact effects, point costs and conflicts, plus the best picks for your second run.
 Probably Stolen By Johan Published Oct 28, 2026 Perks 
 Beginner 
 Starting Perks unlock after your first run. When you start a new store you can pick up to 4 perks and spend up to 3 perk points . Positive perks cost 1 or 2 points. Negative perks give points back, 1 to 4 each, so a drawback can pay for another positive perk. Every perk takes one of your 4 slots either way.
 Colorful Character is the exception to the slot limit: it costs 1 point and gives you 3 more perk slots. That pays off when you stack drawbacks for points, because 4 slots fill up fast once each negative perk is buying you a positive one.
 Some perks can’t be combined. Once you pick one perk of a pair, the other is locked:

 Negative perks
 Each negative perk gives back the points in the first column. Take Ascetic (4 points back) and your budget grows to 7 points, enough to fill your other 3 slots with 2-point perks. A few drawbacks carry a small upside, such as Gambling Addiction unlocking all lottery content, but they all make the run harder in some way, so pick ones your play style can absorb.

 Good picks for a second run
 Savings (1 point) is the simplest pick: 150 extra credits on day one, which covers early stock while you learn what sells.
 Model Citizen (1 point) starts you at 30 Security reputation. That already clears the 20 needed for the Security Surplus reputation perk, so a Security Surplus Merchant visits now and then. It also pays 50% more for reporting customers on the Wanted List. On a Fence or Chemist start it won’t get you there alone: on Mark Up (Normal), those specializations open with less Security reputation and pull you back under 20.
 Well Connected (2 points) adds 15 reputation with every faction. That leaves you 5 points short of the +20 perks that five factions offer, which a few good trades will cover.
 Vigilant (2 points) stops night-time robberies completely, which matters most once your store holds valuable stock.
