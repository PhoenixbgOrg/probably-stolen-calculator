Points and slots
The current new-game sequence is occupation → perks → difficulty and options. Perks are disabled on the first run. Later starts must pass all checks when continuing:
Check
Required condition
Points
Sum of signed perk costs ≤3; negative costs offset positive costs
Slots
Selected count ≤4 plus all selected extra slots; 0-cost perks still use one slot
Incompatibility
A declaration on either side blocks the pair
Selections may temporarily exceed a limit; Continue performs validation. Colorful Character is neutral, costs +1, occupies a slot and adds 3 slots, raising the total limit to 7. Category is not determined by cost sign.
Application order
Order
Settlement
1
Initialize base state and activate the selected perk list; Minimalist/Ascetic change inventory dimensions, and substance-addiction timers initialize
2
Place Hoarder's blocking debris before starting stock
3
Grant occupation stock, change occupation cash/rent and apply occupation reputation changes; then process inputs for Sentinel, addictions and the welfare introduction
4
If skipping the introduction, unlock the relevant introductions/services and grant documents/tools, keeping Rock Bottom and perk-specific exclusions
5
Apply remaining starting effects: cash, rent, direct reputation changes, tapes, toolbox, reactor, luck, vending machine and others; then save the new run
Selected perks already affect persistent checks during occupation-stock initialization. Ordinary occupation reputation changes first receive Blabbermouth, Sycophant, difficulty and other applicable modifiers. Perks' own direct starting reputation adjustments occur afterward.

Modifiers and rounding
These are perk-specific modifiers. The complete trade, fee, faction, health and security rules remain in their respective topics.
Perk
Stage and order
Diplomatic
Positive trade-reputation input is rounded after ×1.33, then divided by 400 before ordinary reputation settlement; not an unconditional 33% increase to final reputation
Blabbermouth
Ordinary positive Black Market and Revolutionary reputation gains ×0.5; negative changes are not halved
Sycophant
Ordinary positive Upper Level reputation gains ×1.25; adds 10% of the customer's original budget alongside other original-budget bonuses
Intimidating + Blabbermouth
Crime evidence is rounded after ×0.67, then rounded again after ×1.33; the two modifiers do not exactly cancel
Convict / Model Citizen
Affected report cash, Security reputation inputs and Aug-kill rewards use ×0.75 / ×1.5; not limited to wanted-client reports
Aug Menace
Aug-kill cash receives another ×1.25 and rounding after the preceding modifier; introduces events early and retains the run-ending risk at presence 100
Awful Negotiator
Market-stage +5 percentage points buying and −5 selling; also hides bargaining controls
Substance withdrawal
Each active substance withdrawal: market-stage +8 points buying and −8 selling; the three can stack with one another and with Awful Negotiator
Wild's Favorite / On Wild's Bad Side
Network upgrades ×0.9 / ×1.1 before the network's final rounding; insurance gets a final rounded ×0.5 / ×1.5 after its other pricing
Side Street / Prime Real Estate
Normal random-customer target −1 / +1, finally clamped to 0–6; actual total visitors need not differ by exactly 1
Short Sleeper
Only removes the outing-related −1 normal-customer target penalty; other limits remain
Agoraphobia
After an actual outing on day D, the next is allowed on D+3, leaving two intervening nights
Proficient / Careful Scavenger and Osteoarthritis
Start with 5 attempts, apply +2/−2, then subtract completed weeks since day 1 for Osteoarthritis; if below 3, scavenging stops before event bonuses. Maimed returns 0 earlier
Tinkerer
At upgrade-consumption calls wired to the perk, each material has a 20% retention roll; this is not proof that every upgrade or consumable uses it
The explicitly rounded integer stages use nearest-integer rounding with midpoint ties to even. Intermediate rounding matters, so consecutive percentage modifiers cannot simply be added. Ordinary reputation itself can retain fractions; this rounding rule does not cover every reputation operation.
Substance-use timers increase nightly and can naturally remove their corresponding perk at 20; narcotic withdrawal additionally checks its relief timer. Sentinel's 7/10/14-day thresholds, disease pool, treatment and full addiction state rules belong to

Welfare Program
Processed when opening on days congruent to 1 modulo 7. Secret Admirer runs first, so successfully placed owned gifts may affect the same opening's welfare valuation.
Let cash be C and the game's total estimated value be T. Adjusted wealth is 
W = C + trunc((T−C)/3)
, dividing as an integer toward zero before adding cash. T includes cash and the trade estimates of eligible owned items in the main inventory, both counters, display, document area, trash inventory and both vending areas. Container valuation may include children, while locked items and other cases can be excluded. It is not a simple sum of catalog base values or all assets.

Gambling Addiction's compulsive purchases
Only qualifying customer-owned items successfully placed on the front counter through direct selling trigger this rule; barter is excluded. Lottery tickets have one counter. Single scratch tickets and scratch-ticket stacks share another counter. Both reset for a new customer; only successful purchases increment them. Failed attempts can be retried by the next qualifying item, with at most 5 successful purchases per category per customer.

Hoarder
Before occupation stock, the game attempts to place rest, tool and service debris piles at the bottom of the main inventory. They cannot be moved directly. A failed preset placement falls back to ordinary placement.
Pile
Clearing condition
Progress output
Rest pile
10 nights without an actual outing; an outing pauses that night without resetting progress, so the nights need not be consecutive
Each qualifying night attempts 1 Scrap Metal and 1 ordinary Dumping Ground draw to the back counter
Tool pile
Use a crowbar, wire cutter and plasma cutter on this pile once each; both tool and pile must be owned; each tool ID counts once
Each tool is destroyed; attempts 3 Common Electronic Parts, Wire and Scrap Metal respectively to the main inventory; third distinct tool clears the pile immediately
Service pile
10 nights with the unlocked debris-removal service enabled; outings do not pause it
Base service fee 50 Credits per active night, with 5 ordinary Dumping Ground draws to the back counter
Clearing any pile additionally makes 5 ordinary Dumping Ground draws and 5 draws excluding ordinary junk. The tool pile sends clearing output to the main inventory; the other two use the back counter. A completing overnight pile grants that night's progress output before clearing output. Draws can be empty and placement can fail, so this is not a promise of 10 items. Full loot tables belong to 
Afterhours and Scavenging
.

Device edits are immediate and closing the window does not undo them. See the 
Mind Reprogrammer
 for the complete list, charge costs, per-device cooldown and acquisition.
Blackmail demands 40 + current day Credits. Refusal or arrest removes the perk and passes −30 to each of the four existing Lower Levels, Revolution, Security and Upper Levels reputation records, subject to reputation modifiers. The code also calls −30 for Neural Church, but this build does not create that reputation record in a new run, so that call has no actual reputation effect. Visits, choices and original dialogue belong to

trunc(day/5) + 30
, with Black Market distrust still able to double it. Full service rules belong to
