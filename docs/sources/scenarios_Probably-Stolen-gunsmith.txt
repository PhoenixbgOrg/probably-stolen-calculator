 Quick Guide
 Gunsmith is a weapon-focused specialization built around buying, modding, and selling firearms.
 Permit system : Your firearm permit requires security reputation of at least 8 or it gets revoked.
 Custom orders pay big: match firepower, accuracy, ergonomics, or style thresholds for large payouts.

 Avoid the gambling perk : it blocks guide reading and pairs poorly with hard mode restrictions.
 Gunsmith Overview: What the Specialization Does
 The Gunsmith specialization in Probably Stolen transforms your shop into a firearms workshop. You gain a dedicated gunsmith storage cabinet for firearms and parts, the ability to take guns apart with a screwdriver, a 3D printer for fabricating components like plastic pistol stocks, and access to the custom gun order market. It also unlocks melee-adjacent crafting such as kitchen knives and box cutters from scrap.
 The core loop is simple: buy cheap guns, strip them for parts, upgrade weapons with attachments, display them in your window to attract gun-buying customers, and fulfill custom build orders for premium payouts.

 Permits and Security Reputation
 Your firearm permit is the gate to the whole specialization. It is normally reserved for upper-level merchants and lets you legally trade mid- to high-level contraband. However, it comes with strict conditions.

 Keep Security Happy
 Selling contraband while security reputation is negative invites patrols and fines. Selling medicine and supplies to officers on patrol, and reporting actual criminals, are reliable ways to build the reputation your permit needs.
 Custom Orders and Gun Modding Step-by-Step
 Custom orders are the highest-earning activity for a Gunsmith. Customers request a weapon meeting specific stat thresholds — firepower, accuracy, ergonomics, recoil control, reliability, or style. Attachments visibly change the gun's appearance, so experiment freely.
 1
 Accept the Right Orders
 Check which stat the customer wants and whether you can realistically hit the threshold. Orders listing multiple required stats need all of them satisfied before you can accept payment.
 2
 Source Parts and Materials
 Buy from the Retired Gunsmith, scavenge gun parts, or craft components. Upgrade parts often require metal ingots — smelt scrap in a furnace and use metal printer chips for 3D-printed components like stocks.
 3
 Strip Donor Weapons
 Use a screwdriver to take cheap or broken guns apart. Salvaged barrels, springs, and receivers feed your upgrade bench instead of your trash can.
 4
 Mod to Threshold
 Attach parts targeting the requested stat. Makeshift red dots raise accuracy but lower other stats — watch the trade-offs so you don't tank a secondary requirement.
 5
 Deliver and Upsell
 Complete the order, then offer ammo and accessories. Gun buyers frequently want rounds, melee weapons, and personal protection gear in the same visit.
 Stat Common Boosting Parts Trade-off 
 Firepower Improved springs, receiver parts Often costly in materials

 Recoil control Compensators, grips Minimal downsides 
 Ergonomics Laser pointers, grips Needs electrical parts

 Efficiency and Performance
 When upgrading your workbench and printer, prioritize performance and efficiency upgrades — they speed up part fabrication, which is your bottleneck for fulfilling multiple custom orders per day.

 FAQ
 Q: How do I unlock the Gunsmith firearm permit in Probably Stolen?
 The permit is normally reserved for upper-level merchants. As a Gunsmith, it activates once you reach at least 8 security reputation, and it will be revoked again if your security reputation drops below zero.
 Q: Why can't I accept a custom gun order payment?
 Custom orders list specific stat requirements such as firepower, accuracy, ergonomics, or recoil control. You must satisfy all listed thresholds simultaneously before the accept option becomes available — hitting only one stat is not enough.
 Q: What are metal printer chips used for?
 Metal printer chips power the metal printing process, letting you fabricate upgraded gun parts and 3D-printed components like pistol stocks. Pair them with a furnace and scrap metal to produce metal ingots for high-tier upgrades.
 Q: Is the gambling perk worth taking as a Gunsmith?
 Generally no. The gambling perk prevents you from reading any in-game guides, which severely limits appraisal accuracy and custom order planning. It also conflicts with scavenge-related perks that lock each other out.
