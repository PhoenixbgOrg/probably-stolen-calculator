 Probably Stolen Starting Specializations Guide
 Every starting specialization in Probably Stolen, the credits, rent and gear each one gives you, and which start to pick first.
 Probably Stolen By Johan Published Oct 28, 2026 Beginner 
 Specializations 
 Your specialization decides how many credits you open with, what rent you start paying, what’s already in your store and which trade you can run from day one. Take Apprentice on your first run. The start screen marks it as recommended for new players, and its stock is ordinary food and household goods that sell without special equipment.
 You can pick from nine specializations. The difficulty you pick doesn’t change your starting credits or your kit. It does scale the reputation some specializations start with, as each section below shows.
 Starting Perks stack on top of your specialization from your second run onward. Savings , for example, adds 150 credits to whatever your specialization gives you. The Starting Perks guide lists them all.
 What every start includes
 Every specialization opens with the Lease Agreement for your store in your Dossier. Every start except Rock Bottom also puts four corrupted music cassettes in the cassette player: High Stakes , Stardust , Afterglow and Spaceflight . Take the Tapehead perk and you get the full cassette set instead.
 On Price Gouge (Cozy) and Mark Up (Normal), you also start with Rent Grace already owned. It lets you miss one rent payment and pay it together with the next week’s rent. On harder difficulties you have to buy it from Joe Wild.

 Apprentice
 You open with 550 credits , a Storage Bay and a small mixed stock. The stock is worth 144 credits at base value, so your first customers have something to buy before you restock.
 You start with:
 Storage Bay (Shabby)
 2 × Peat
 Puice
 2 × Tube of Toothpaste
 2 × Roll of Toilet Paper
 Skipping the intro also adds a Food Neon Sign and a Household Item Neon Sign.
 The start screen promises Apprentice extra credits, but it opens with 50 fewer than Rat Wrangler. What you really gain is the Storage Bay and the stock.
 Water Merchant
 You open with 400 credits and a kit built around testing water and cleaning it before you sell it.
 You start with:
 Water Trading Manual
 Water Testing Kit, full of Water Testing Strips
 Bottle of Water Purification Tablets, full
 Bottle of Water (Large), filled with Ghostwater
 Jug of Water, filled with Rustwater
 Bottle of Water (Large)
 Bottle of Water (Standard)
 Bottle of Water (Small)
 Water Pitcher
 Water Filter
 HydraTech Micro Water Purifier (Shabby, at 75 of 300 durability)
 Bottle Printer (Shabby) holding:
 Energy Cell
 Skipping the intro also adds a Water Neon Sign.
 The Water Trading network upgrade is already unlocked, so you don’t have to buy it from Joe Wild.
 Mislabeled water is the main way this start loses money.
 Fence
 You open with 450 credits , a Smuggler’s Bay and a small stock of contraband. This start leans on the Black Market from the first day, and Security already distrusts you.
 Your starting rent is 250 credits , 50 more than the usual 200.
 You start with:
 Smuggler’s Bay (Shabby)
 2 × Vial of “Dream Dust”
 Ironcraft 10 (Fair condition)
 2 × 10mm Ammunition
 Disposable Keycard (Medical)
 Disposable Keycard (Engineering)
 Disposable Keycard (Security)
 2 × Black Lamp (Shabby)
 The Underground Exchange is open from day one.
 Your starting reputation shifts with the difficulty you pick:
 Faction | Price Gouge (Cozy) | Mark Up (Normal) | Thin Margins (Hard) | Red Ink (Challenge) | 
 Black Market | +45 | +30 | +22.5 | +15 | 
 Security | -15 | -30 | -37.5 | -60 | 
 The intro isn’t available for this specialization.
 Keep an eye on Security. You start in the red with them.
 Rock Bottom
 You open with 0 credits , no cassettes, no stock and no equipment. This is the hardest start, so save it for a run where you already know the economy.
 The intro isn’t available for this specialization.
 Hydroponic Farmer
 You open with 450 credits and worn-out Hydroponic Systems, with seeds and nutrient tablets to get them growing.
 Your starting rent is 240 credits , 40 more than the usual 200.
 You start with:
 Hydroponic Farming Manual
 Old Hydroponic System (Shabby)
 Decrepit Hydroponic System (Shabby) holding:
 Energy Cell
 Bottle of Water (Large), filled with Ghostwater
 2 × Eco Module (can’t be removed)
 2 × Ruined Module (can’t be removed)
 2 × Sealed Water Ration (Small)
 2 × Nutrifruit Seed
 Hydroponic Nutrient Tablet Bottle, full
 2 × Fillerweed Seed
 The Retired Farmer network upgrade is already unlocked, so you don’t have to buy it from Joe Wild.
 Gunsmith
 You open with 150 credits and a store full of guns, ammunition and gun mods.
 Your starting rent is 250 credits , 50 more than the usual 200.
 You start with:
 3D Printer
 Firearm Neon Sign
 4 × 3D Printing Filament
 Gunsmith Storage Bay (Shabby)
 6 × .22 SR Ammunition (Non-Lethal)
 5 × .22 SR Ammunition
 Nestfire Compact (Poor condition)
 Nestfire Compact (Good condition)
 Liberator 3D Printed Pistol (Poor condition)
 Liberator 3D Printed Pistol (New condition)
 Suppressor
 Red Dot Sight
 2 × .22 Magazine
 High Capacity .22 Magazine
 Toy Laser Pointer
 Makeshift Suppressor
 Laser Sight
 Improved Internal Springs
 Screwdriver
 Class I Weapon Permit (in the Display Case)
 Gun Parts
 The Retired Gunsmith and Weapon Permit I network upgrades are already unlocked, so you don’t have to buy them from Joe Wild.
 The Class I Weapon Permit is already granted, so you can trade the guns it covers from day one.
 Your starting reputation shifts with the difficulty you pick:
 Faction | Price Gouge (Cozy) | Mark Up (Normal) | Thin Margins (Hard) | Red Ink (Challenge) | 
 Security | +37.5 | +25 | +18.75 | +12.5 | 
 The intro isn’t available for this specialization.
 Cash is tight here: after Rock Bottom, Gunsmith and Aquafarmer open with the fewest credits of any start. Sell some of the spare guns and mods early to cover your first rent. On Price Gouge (Cozy) and Mark Up (Normal), the Security reputation you start with clears the 20 needed for Security Surplus , so a Security Surplus Merchant visits now and then.
 Chemist
 You open with 300 credits and a full chemistry setup: lab machines, droppers, graduated cylinders and the Chemistry Manual.
 You start with:
 Blender (Shabby)
 Heating Plate (Shabby)
 Evaporator (Shabby), with an Energy Cell
 Chem Finisher (Shabby)
 1ml Dropper
 5ml Dropper
 25ml Graduated Cylinder
 50ml Graduated Cylinder
 100ml Graduated Cylinder
 2 × Bottle of Water (Standard)
 Coffee Beans
 Water Pitcher
 Kotton Fabric
 Composition Spectrometer (Shabby)
 Mini Smuggler’s Bay (Shabby)
 Chemistry Manual
 The Retired Chemist network upgrade is already unlocked, so you don’t have to buy it from Joe Wild.
 The Cartel Protection nightly service is unlocked, and this is the only start where the Sector 13 Cartel faction exists.
 Your starting reputation shifts with the difficulty you pick:
 Faction | Price Gouge (Cozy) | Mark Up (Normal) | Thin Margins (Hard) | Red Ink (Challenge) | 
 Security | -7.5 | -15 | -18.75 | -30 | 
 The intro isn’t available for this specialization.
 Rat Wrangler
 You open with 600 credits and a breeding herd of rats, with the food to keep them going.
 You start with:
 Animal Cage (Shabby), with a Livestock Feed Dispenser
 3 × Rat (Male)
 3 × Rat (Female)
 Meat Cleaver
 Blue Labeler
 Marking Pen
 Livestock Immunity Serum (2 uses left)
 6 × Small Nutrifruit
 The Ranching network upgrade is already unlocked, so you don’t have to buy it from Joe Wild.
 Aquafarmer
 You open with 150 credits , fish farming systems and Tilapia to breed, plus roe and feed to grow the stock.
 Your starting rent is 240 credits , 40 more than the usual 200.
 You start with:
 2 × Recirculating Aquaculture System (Shabby) each holding:
 RAS Water Tank
 Energy Cell
 Tilapia (Male)
 Tilapia (Female)
 Fertilized Tilapia Roe
 2 × Industrial Fish Feed
 Scrap Metal
 Electronic Parts (Common)
 Kitchen Knife
 Marking Pen
 2 × Jug of Water, filled with Ghostwater
 The Aquaculture network upgrade is already unlocked, so you don’t have to buy it from Joe Wild.
 Work In Progress specializations
 Junker, Organ Dealer and Street Food Vendor appear on the start screen with the Work In Progress tag, but you can’t start a run with them yet.
